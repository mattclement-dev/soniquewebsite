Bio

Who is SONIQUE?

More about her story.

Independent singer-songwriter SONIQUE is a dynamic vocal powerhouse whose music blends raw emotion, infectious energy and authentic storytelling.

Known for her captivating stage presence and heartfelt songwriting, she creates music that resonates deeply with audiences, taking listeners on a journey through happiness, heartbreak, passion and self-discovery.

Her distinctive alternative electro-pop sound, infused with an edgy and soulful twist, has established her as a bold new voice in South African music. Since the release of her debut single, 'Why Are We Still Here', in 2024, she has continued to build momentum with releases including 'Marinade', 'Finding Your Way', 'My Calling', 'Uncaged', 'Black & White' and most recently 'Friends' in 2026.

Alongside national and international media exposure and a growing live performance presence, SONIQUE remains passionate about creating music that inspires connection, healing and hope. Her powerful vocals, magnetic performances and emotionally honest songwriting leave a lasting impact long after the final note.
