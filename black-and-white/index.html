Black & White

Rising artist SONIQUE releases another new single, ‘Black & White’ – a raw and relatable track for those who know what it’s like to live inside an overthinking mind.

‘Black & White’ is a conversation with the mind – the longing for peace colliding with the restless circle of thoughts that refuse to switch off. SONIQUE captures the feeling of being chased by something unseen, caught in the endless loop of ‘what ifs’, where nothing feels clear.

“When you overthink, nothing feels black and white,” says SONIQUE. “There’s no simple yes or no, right or wrong – only endless shades of grey. It’s a constant ‘what if,’ where you’re never fully at ease, yet never completely uneasy either.”

She continues to explain, “I wrote ‘Black & White’ for those chasing a calm mind but end up feeling stuck, to remind them they’re seen, heard and not alone.”
