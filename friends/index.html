Friends

Rising artist SONIQUE releases another powerful new single, ‘Friends’ – a bold reminder of the importance of self-worth and the danger of losing yourself in the shadow of others.

‘Friends’ – is liberation with attitude. Transforming disappointment into empowerment. While the track explores themes of fake friendships and betrayal, it’s not about sadness. Instead, it stands as an anthem of growth, resilience and self-respect, a declaration of outgrowing negativity and walking away stronger.

“Some friendships come and go like forest fires, some fast some slow – in each one there’s a lesson that helps shape you into the best version of yourself,” says SONIQUE. “The shift for me was realizing I didn’t want to stay in a friendship where someone’s image and control mattered more than respect and care.”

The song draws from a deeply personal experience. “I wrote ‘Friends’ after walking away from a very unfulfilling relationship where I felt like I was constantly giving more than I was receiving,” she explains. “It often felt competitive and one-sided, like I was pouring my energy into someone who didn’t make space for me. I was compared, dismissed and left feeling unsupported.”
