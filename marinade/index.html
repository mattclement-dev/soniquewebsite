Marinade

SONIQUE is a young, vibrant and energetic new artist. Her new single ‘Marinade’ in collaboration with RADA Unearthed is a captivating and inspiring track that centers around how one reacts to personal challenges that life throws at us. It’s about how you overcome problems instead of letting them define you. Finding a positive outcome when there seems to be none and growing from it.

“Marinade is about realising that our darkness is part of the journey that shapes us to be stronger, wiser and powerful beings. One should stay confident, keep believing and discover your purpose,” says SONIQUE.

‘Marinade’ is SONIQUE’s first big release following her single ‘Why Are We Still Here’ which is part of her first album.
