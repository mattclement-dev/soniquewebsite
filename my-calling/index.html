My Calling

SONIQUE returns with a bold and inspiring new single, ‘My Calling’, a song that speaks to the power of self-discovery, honesty and confidence. Through heartfelt lyrics and an uplifting sound, SONIQUE delivers a message of empowerment, urging listeners to embrace their true selves without fear or compromise.

“Too often, we hide in the shadows of others when we’re not feeling our best,” says SONIQUE. “We pretend to be someone we’re not just to fit in. But true strength comes from facing our truth, standing tall and not letting the criticism of others define us. Growth happens when we stop resisting and start embracing the lessons life offers.”

With its inspiring message and dynamic production, ‘My Calling’ is a declaration of self-worth and resilience. Encouraging listeners to step into their power, the track serves as a reminder that challenges can be transformed into opportunities for growth.
