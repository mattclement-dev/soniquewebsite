Together We Are Stronger

The collaborative inspirational track is one where combined energy and passion shine through, creating a powerful new single that resonates with listeners. Its message is one of unity, resilience and collective strength.

‘Together We Are Stronger’ was produced by Mark Beling and written by Neil Engel. It features the voices of several talented artists, including iSandi, Rameo, Pedro Barbosa, Lia Elise, Ross Learmonth, SEVVEN, Me-Ko, SONIQUE, SeanJ and Mark Beling.

The track is included on RADA’s latest album, RADA Unearthed Volume Three, and is their third collaborative campaign song. Its purpose is clear: to spread positivity and inspiration in the world. The song addresses daily personal and societal challenges, reminding us that by standing together, we can overcome anything — we are stronger together. Never give up, never let go.

“Music has the power to connect hearts and minds,” shares John-Paul Nobrega, founder and CEO of RADA. “Our goal is to inspire people to stand up for one another and to know that no matter how tough life gets, we’re stronger together. It’s more than just a song; it’s a movement. It’s about being brave enough to fight for positive change, showing kindness and leading with love. That’s what makes us stronger.”
