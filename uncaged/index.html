Uncaged

SONIQUE makes a powerful return with her new single, ‘Uncaged’ – an emotionally charged original song that captures the raw journey of breaking free. The track describes life; how self-transformation happens when facing and embracing one’s truth and rising above the weight that sometimes holds us down.

‘Uncaged’ speaks to the kind of growth that comes through struggle. Woven through the lyrics is the metaphor of metamorphosis: the challenge of shedding old identities, confronting internal battles, enduring the pain of change and emerging freer, braver, being more aligned with your true self.

The lyrics reflect on the immense amount of strength it takes to let go and begin again. That quiet power that comes from within that allows one to reclaim one’s voice.

SONIQUE emphasises, “It’s a song about transformation, about breaking free from the version of yourself shaped by fear and letting go of what no longer serves you.”

She elaborates by explaining, “I’ve lived through emotions that nearly swallowed me whole, but I refused to let them silence me. Instead of keeping my feelings and the lessons they have taught me locked away, I release them through my music. It’s my way of speaking the truths I once buried, in the hope that someone out there; someone who feels like they have no one to turn to, can find my songs, relate to them and feel even just a little less alone.”
